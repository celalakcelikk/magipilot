# Code of conduct

Magipilot wants to be a friendly project to use and to contribute to, for everyone, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive criticism; discuss the work, not the person.
- Respect different opinions and experience.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break it, and may block someone temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@celalakcelikk](https://github.com/celalakcelikk), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
